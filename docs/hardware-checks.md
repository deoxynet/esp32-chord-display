# Core2 bring-up

The firmware has been compiled for `m5stack-core2` and the portable application logic runs in native tests.

Verified on the attached hardware on 2026-11-13:

- Upload succeeded and the bootloader/application flash hashes verified on an ESP32-D0WDQ6-V3 Core2.
- A real BLE connection from the Mac discovered the standard MIDI service and its read/write/notify characteristic. A test Cmaj7 and its note-offs arrived with no dropped or malformed packets.
- The WIDI Master reported model `CME WIDI Master` and firmware field `0260` through its standard Device Information service.
- Recovered Auto-learn commands were acknowledged, and register readback confirmed AUTO role plus four zeroed address slots. Original settings were backed up before modification.
- After an explicit control-link disconnect, the WIDI Master connected to the Core2 as central. The Core2 remained a peripheral in WIDI group mode and received MIDI packets with no decode errors.
- The user confirmed that playing the physical keyboard displays chord names.
- An explicit group containing the Core2 and Mac addresses was written and read back through the recovered protocol. This saves the group entirely in software.
- The Mac's old outgoing WIDI pairing was removed while MIDI advertising was active. Bluetooth logs then confirmed an incoming WIDI connection. The Mac received active-sensing MIDI while the Core2 simultaneously received packets with zero malformed/dropped packets. A simultaneous played-chord check is pending.
- After restarting the Core2, it automatically rejoined the saved group while the Mac continued receiving active sensing. The longest interval between Mac messages during the captured session was 319 ms.
- The subsequent Harmony/UI update was flashed successfully with verified hashes. It booted with the same BLE identity and retained the saved Harmony view. Logs and original analysis artifacts are retained under the ignored `build/` directory. Full two-device power-cycle testing, extended playing, battery life and every visual/control edge case below remain separate checks.

1. **Boot and orientation:** flash `core2-demo`, confirm an upright 320 × 240 display, then inspect the Chord, Harmony and Staff tabs. Confirm that each front touch button selects the matching view, and that holding C opens settings without a second tap action.
2. **Readability:** view D-13, CΔ7/G, C°7, Bø7 and C7b9 from a normal playing distance. Check the large Roman numeral, bold function name and separate target detail without reducing the main chord size. Check the ten-pixel staff spacing, larger noteheads, ledger lines, accidentals and clefs on the actual panel. Compare normal and reduced brightness. Switch Chord symbols to Standard and back, reboot and check persistence. Confirm idle screens have no generic playing instructions.
3. **Settings and gestures:** change channel, spelling, sustain, brightness, tonic/mode and display mode, reboot and confirm persistence. Demo and freeze should reset. Tap the large Harmony Key / Mode bar at its left, middle and right; also try a longer stationary press. Verify direct touches on all twelve piano-key tonics and eight modes, plus A/B/C navigation. Swipe left/right through all three playing views and up to the key picker from Staff. Compare modes with horizontal swipes inside the picker; swipe down to dismiss. Swipe down from a playing view to open settings, then down again to close it. A diagonal stroke, short jitter, two-finger stroke or footer slide should not navigate. Key selection must not disturb the BLE connection.
   Change tonic between white and black notes: 1 should remain at the same left anchor and degrees 2–7 should ascend toward the octave tonic marked 8. Check that selecting a newly positioned key chooses that pitch, and dim edge fragments cannot change the tonic. Mode changes and incoming MIDI must leave the keyboard position fixed.
4. **Pairing:** flash `core2` and follow the README's software group setup. Check the Core2's address before/after restarts and automatic reconnection without a phone app connected.
5. **Chord input and harmony:** play C–E–G, E–G–C, G–C–E–B and an extended dominant. Confirm chord names, slash bass and the merged interval chips, then compare staff pitches to the keyboard. With D Dorian selected, play Dm7 and add B; then play G/D. With C Major selected, play Dm7–G7–Cmaj7 and inspect the trail/cadence prompt. Single notes and literal fourth stacks should not assert a functional root. Middle C uses MIDI note 60 = C4.
6. **Pedal and channels:** hold CC64, release the keys and confirm blue sustained notes. Retrigger a sustained note, lift the pedal while holding the key, then release. Test the same pitch on two channels and an explicit channel filter. Select the instrument channel if other group traffic or drum notes would otherwise be merged into the chord.
   With the live scale reference open, play single notes and chords: white held keys, shaded pedal notes, per-note degrees and the role readout should follow the performance. In D Dorian, try B (6/color), C# (7/outside) and Bb (b6/outside). Change octaves and release one of two E notes; a still-held octave must keep E highlighted. Playing MIDI must leave the selected tonic unchanged.
7. **Recovery:** hold a chord, disconnect the WIDI and confirm the display clears/unfreezes, then reconnect and play. Hold B to clear locally stuck notes. Verify CC120 (all sound off), CC121 (reset controllers) and CC123 (all notes off, respecting sustain).
8. **Dense traffic:** send fast chords with MIDI clock/active sensing enabled and large SysEx messages in between. Confirm responsive touch, no stuck notes and no growing `DROP`/`BAD` counters in settings. `DROP` indicates receive-queue overflow; `BAD` indicates malformed BLE framing. An overflow displays a short recovery notice and clears locally tracked notes.
9. **Latency and longevity:** measure recognition and screen-transfer time on the ESP32, then leave a real MIDI session running for several hours. Desktop timing is not an ESP32 latency measurement. Check reconnections and free heap before claiming stage reliability or battery life.

The firmware permits one BLE MIDI connection. Group mode makes the Core2 a peripheral; optional Auto/Direct modes also support initiating a client connection to a WIDI peripheral. It receives note and controller events, ignores pitch bend for display naming, and tracks CC64 sustain. It does not implement MIDI-thru, outgoing performance MIDI, USB MIDI, notation durations, key detection or per-instrument separation inside an already merged single-channel stream.
